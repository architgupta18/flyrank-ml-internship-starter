# What I Am Proving — Week 1

I can turn content search-performance data into a transparent, evidence-backed page-review priority queue. I am trying to persuade a content analytics manager who is hiring a junior data/ML analyst for a search or content team. My portfolio will show a small number of case studies that make the work inspectable: the decision being improved, the data used, a simple baseline, the evidence, and the limitations. The single action I want from that manager is an invitation to interview for a junior data/ML analyst role.

**Why this needs to exist:** A CV or LinkedIn profile can list Python and ML coursework, but it cannot show how I reason from imperfect data to a cautious, usable recommendation; an owned portfolio can show the evidence, validation, and limits directly.

## Evidence boundary

This is a focused, provisional claim based on my FlyRank ML internship work. I will not claim that a queue causes traffic growth or that I have production experience I cannot demonstrate. Each case study must distinguish observed patterns from causal claims and make its data and validation limits clear.
